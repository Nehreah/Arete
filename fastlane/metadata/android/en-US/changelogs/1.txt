Initial release of Aréte:
- Sovereign, decentralized local social network (Bluetooth LE & Wi-Fi Direct mesh).
- End-to-end encryption (E2EE) with TOFU cryptographic verification.
- Digital letters, proximity radar, and public/private profiles.
- OpenStreetMap integration and native GPS positioning without tracking.
- 100% free of trackers and proprietary services (GPLv3).
